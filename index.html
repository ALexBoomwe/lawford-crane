FAQ

Questions Owners Ask.

Do I get paid if the property is empty?

+

Yes. Under the fixed rent model, you receive the agreed rent whether the property is occupied or empty. Occupancy risk sits with us.

Do you charge a management fee?

+

No. We do not deduct a monthly management fee from your agreed rent. On a £4,500/month property, a typical 12% management fee would cost £540 per month or £6,480 per year.

Can I receive rent upfront?

+

For selected central London properties, we can structure multi-year upfront rent payments. This is subject to property assessment, income modelling and legal review.

Is every property eligible?

+

No. We review each property individually based on location, condition, legal structure, operating potential and commercial viability.

Do you only work with overseas landlords?

+

No. We work with UK-based landlords, overseas owners, investors, portfolio landlords and second-home owners.

Who handles maintenance?

+

We coordinate day-to-day maintenance, contractors and urgent operational issues as part of the agreed structure.

What types of properties do you prefer?

+

We focus mainly on qualifying prime central London apartments and houses, especially Mayfair, Belgravia, Kensington, Chelsea, Marylebone, St John's Wood and surrounding prime locations.
